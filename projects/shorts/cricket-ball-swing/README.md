# Why a Cricket Ball Swings

- **Format:** YouTube Short (vertical)
- **Status:** Planned
- **Topic:** Conventional swing bowling

## Story

Explain why a cricket ball curves in the air. The animation should show the bowler releasing a ball with its seam angled, the contrast between a smooth/shiny side and a rough side, and the resulting airflow imbalance that creates sideways force.

## Key beats

1. The ball travels straight from the bowler's hand.
2. The seam is angled toward slips or fine leg.
3. Air stays smoother on one side and becomes more turbulent on the other.
4. The pressure imbalance pushes the ball sideways: swing.
5. Finish with a clear inswing versus outswing comparison.
